01 / Background

A researcher with a practical point of view.

My work sits at the intersection of rigorous research, international business, and decision-ready communication, with a strong interest in people, behaviour, and the patterns behind decisions.

I have delivered research across academic, commercial, and policy-facing contexts, from a large-scale PhD project on business networks to strategy research for high-growth SMEs. Earlier work in the international exhibition sector gave me direct experience of client consultation, market positioning, project planning, and the realities behind business decisions.

Today, I bring those perspectives together: designing robust studies, making sense of qualitative and quantitative evidence, adopting AI as a research support tool, and presenting the result in a way that helps people act. I use AI alongside source verification, critical review, and human judgement rather than as a substitute for research rigour.

02 / Research

How relationships shape business.

My doctoral research examined how guanxi networks change under different levels of international exposure in China’s exhibition industry.

Doctoral research

University of Greenwich Mixed methods Social network analysis

Guanxi Networks within the Chinese Exhibition Industry: Differences in International Exposure

The project combined 45 semi-structured interviews, ego-network questionnaires, and non-participant observations to investigate how international business exposure affects relationship-building, trust, brokerage, and decision-making.

Finding 01

Network structures influence partnership choices and market behaviour.

Finding 02

Brokerage can create competitive advantage in international markets.

Contribution

A model linking international exposure to guanxi network behaviour.
